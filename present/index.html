The Problem

Gas distribution
is a 24/7 logistics puzzle.

Hundreds of cylinders. Three branches. One salesman per route. Daily morning loads, evening returns, customer credits, debt collection, and price rules — all on paper, or in five disconnected spreadsheets.

  • Lost cylinders, missed returns, unaccounted empties.
  • Invoice-vs-reconciliation drift at the end of every day.
  • No single audit trail across branches and roles.
The Product

One system. Every cylinder.
Every branch. Every role.

A role-aware ERP that follows the cylinder from the warehouse, to the loader, to the salesman, to the customer — and back. Built for Oman. Built for the field.

The Loader

One morning hand-off.
One evening reconciliation.

  • Morning: deduct from branch inventory, lock the route for the day.
  • Evening: enter returns, flag discrepancy, manager approves & restocks.
  • Every movement is a CylinderMovement row — counted, not guessed.
The Manager

Close the day, not the books.

  • Branch-scoped KPIs at a glance.
  • Collect debt, write off, approve discrepancies — all with audit trails.
  • Price rules with closed/open windows, branch by branch.
The Admin

Full system control.

The General Manager

Govern every branch
without losing the local picture.

  • GM users directory mirrors the admin console — same RBAC, same audit.
  • Branches, products, roles, finance, reconciliation across all sites.
  • Tokenized customer statement sharing for finance reviews.
The Money

Counted three ways.
Always reconciled.

  • All amounts in Prisma.Decimal(12,3) — 3-decimal OMR (bz).
  • paid + debt == total, enforced at audit boundary.
  • Write-offs are real ledger rows — they reconcile debt without faking cash.
The Device

Mobile-first.
Desktop-equal.

Every screen collapses cleanly to a phone. The 2-panel login folds to one. Tables stack. The 1st & 2nd priority is the field — not the office.

The Trust

Security is not a feature.
It's the foundation.

  • JWT in httpOnly cookies, 12-hour expiry, sessionVersion invalidation on every privilege change.
  • MFA (TOTP) with one-time bcrypt-hashed recovery codes — recoverable from the login form.
  • Route-permission gate tested 385 / 385 across every role and path.
  • Account lockout after 5 failed logins, FAILED_LOGIN audited.
  • Branch-scope: branchless users see nothing. Cross-branch orders are blocked.
