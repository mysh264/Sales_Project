The Problem

Gas distribution is a 24/7 logistics puzzle.

Hundreds of cylinders. Three branches. One salesman per route. Daily morning loads, evening returns, customer credits, and debt collection — usually on paper, or in five disconnected spreadsheets.

  • Lost cylinders, missed returns, unaccounted empties.
  • Invoice-vs-reconciliation drift at the end of every day.
  • No single audit trail across branches and roles.
The Product

One system. Every cylinder. Every branch. Every role.

A role-aware ERP that follows the cylinder from the warehouse, to the loader, to the salesman, to the customer — and back.

The Loader

One morning hand-off. One evening reconciliation.

  • Morning: deduct from branch inventory, lock the route for the day.
  • Evening: enter returns, flag discrepancy, manager approves and restocks.
  • Every movement is a CylinderMovement row — counted, not guessed.
The Manager

Close the day, not the books.

  • Branch-scoped KPIs at a glance.
  • Collect debt, write off, approve discrepancies — all with audit trails.
  • Price rules with closed/open windows, branch by branch.
The Admin

Full system control.

  • Users, branches, products, roles, cylinders, finance, reconciliation.
  • Create Employee requires a branch — no more branchless orphans.
  • Audit logs with diff snapshots, exportable for compliance.
The General Manager

Govern every branch without losing the local picture.

  • GM users directory mirrors the admin console — same RBAC, same audit.
  • Branches, products, roles, finance, reconciliation across all sites.
  • Tokenized customer statement sharing for finance reviews.
The Money

Counted three ways. Always reconciled.

  • All currency amounts use Prisma.Decimal(12,3) before dependent calculations.
  • paid + written off + debt == total, checked against the live database.
  • Write-offs are real ledger rows — they reconcile debt without faking cash.
The Device
