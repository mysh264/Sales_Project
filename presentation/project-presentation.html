Why this exists

The three leaks every cylinder business has

🫥 Missing cylinders
Between the warehouse and the customer, cylinders disappear — and no one can say where, or whose route.
💸 Cash leaks
Hand-written invoices, cash collected but not recorded, debt that no one can reconcile at month-end.
🕳️ No accountability
When something is wrong, there is no record of who changed it, when, or from where.

This platform closes all three — and proves it with data.

Trust & Control

Who can see what — and it's all logged

Right access, per role
Five built-in roles plus customizable permission profiles. A salesman sees his routes; a manager sees the branch; an admin sees everything. Branch data is scoped automatically.

Strong password hashing, multi-factor login with recovery codes, and lockout after repeated failed attempts.
Every action is recorded
Create, change or delete — the system logs who, what, before/after, and from where. Disputes are resolved in seconds.
